How a project works

Small, focused phases. You see working software early and steer the next step with real use instead of guesses.

  1. Weeks 1-2

    Discovery and mapping

    We map your current process: where time leaks, where errors happen, where work gets repeated. You get a clear scope and a fixed quote.

  2. Weeks 3-6

    Build the core

    A working app that handles your core workflow, not a demo. Your team starts using it while we refine it with their feedback.

  3. After launch

    Launch and improve

    We handle deployment, data migration and training, then keep improving it as needed, or hand it over with documentation.

Timelines are typical for a first version; we confirm yours after discovery.

Questions

How much does a custom project cost?

It depends on scope. After a short discovery conversation you get a written, fixed quote for the first version before any work starts. Smaller jobs and consulting sessions can be billed hourly.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes, that is most of what we do. Our tools are built for teams that have outgrown spreadsheets but don't need, or want to pay for, enterprise software.

Should we build custom or buy something off the shelf?

Often buying is the right answer, and we'll tell you if it is. Custom makes sense when your process is what sets you apart, or when you are paying for several tools and gluing them together by hand.

Can you work with the systems we already use?

Usually. We connect to existing tools through their APIs, exports or databases, so a new app can fit alongside what you have instead of replacing everything at once.

What happens after launch?

You can keep us on for hosting, support and improvements, or take it over yourself. Either way you get documentation and a handover.
